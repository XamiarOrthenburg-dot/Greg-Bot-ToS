Greg Bot - Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Greg Bot

Last Updated: September 18, 2026

Your privacy and server security are our top priorities. This Privacy Policy explains how Greg Bot ("the Bot") processes, handles, and secures data when added to your Discord server. Because Greg Bot functions primarily as an anti-raid and security bot, we only process the data required to defend your server against malicious attacks, automated raids, and spam.

1. Data Processed and Collected

To identify and mitigate raid threats in real-time, Greg Bot temporarily processes or logs the following information via the Discord API:

  • Server Data: Guild (Server) IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, and server configuration settings.
  • User Identifiers: Discord User IDs, usernames, and account creation dates (used to detect newly created automated "alt" accounts joining in mass numbers).
  • Gateway Activity: Rapid join rates, repetitive message patterns, and aggressive mention counts.
  • Moderation Action Logs: Records of automated bans, kicks, or timeouts executed by the Bot to defend your server.

2. How We Use Your Data

Data processed by Greg Bot is used exclusively to provide anti-raid protection and server security. We do not track user behavior across channels, harvest personal data, or sell, trade, or share any information with third-party advertisers.

3. Data Retention and Storage

  • Security Logs & Flags: Real-time message logs used to track automated spam patterns are kept temporarily in volatile memory and are cleared quickly once the threat has passed.
  • Server Settings: Anti-raid custom thresholds, log channel settings, and whitelist data are securely stored in our database.

4. Data Deletion Requests

Server owners and users can request the complete erasure of their stored moderation profile history or server settings by contacting the development team directly.

5. Compliance with Discord

Greg Bot operates in strict compliance with the official Discord Developer Terms of Service and Developer Policy.
