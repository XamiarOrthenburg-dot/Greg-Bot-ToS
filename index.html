🛡️ Greg Bot Terms of Service

Last updated: September 18, 2026

1. Authorization & Permission

By adding Greg Bot to your server, you explicitly grant it permission to monitor guild activities, audit logs, public messages, and member profiles. You confirm that you are an administrator or have explicit authority from the server owner to authorize these automated security operations.

2. Security Scanning & Data Processing

To protect your server from malicious raids, spammers, and compromised accounts, Greg Bot processes user IDs, message patterns, and account creation dates. This data is evaluated in real-time. Malicious activity triggers automated moderation responses (such as bans, kicks, or timeouts) based on your configured server settings.

3. Log Retention & Anti-Abuse

Security and logging event data are securely cached by Greg Bot to prevent verification bypasses and bot abuse. We do not inspect, look at, or store private conversational content outside of tracking standard automated security triggers (e.g., spam detection, blacklisted phrases, or invite links).

4. Limitations of Service

While Greg Bot serves as an advanced defense layer, we do not guarantee 100% prevention of all security incidents, raids, or rogue user actions. The service is provided "as-is", and the Greg Bot development team holds no liability for bypassed filters, accidental configuration lockouts, or unmitigated attacks.
